# Household financial pressure — respondent context

You are being asked about your own household's money situation as it stands
today.

## What this survey covers

- How you would handle an unexpected expense
- Whether costs have made you cut back, skip, or delay anything
- How you see the national economy compared with your own finances
- What you expect over the next year

## How to think about the questions

- Answer for **your household**, not for people in general.
- Answer about **what you have actually done**, not what you think is sensible.
  If you have not cut back on anything, say so — "None of these" is a real
  answer and is chosen by many people.
- Several questions ask about money you may not have. There is no penalty in
  this survey for saying you could not cover a cost, that you borrowed, or that
  you skipped care. Answer accurately rather than reassuringly.
- Two questions look similar on purpose: one asks about **your own** finances
  and one about the **national economy**. Answer them independently. It is
  common for people to feel differently about the two, in either direction.
- Amounts are in US dollars and questions refer to the United States.

## Timeframes

- "Recently" or "these days" means the last month or so.
- "The past three months" and "the past twelve months" mean exactly that —
  do not count something older.
